Mr Howard Yu

Mr Howard Yu founded 1Academy in 2011, bringing with him a distinguished career in education. A former MOE teacher and official PSLE Science OEQ Marker, he also served as the first Science Head of Department at the Shanghai-Singapore International School in 2004. He is the author of both a P5/P6 Science OEQ guidebook and a P5/P6 English Character Education guidebook.

"I don't just teach Science — I teach students how to think scientifically, answer precisely, and walk into any exam with confidence."

Mr Yu's approach combines structured concept mastery with targeted exam technique. Over the last decade of coaching, his students have consistently made remarkable improvements — from D to B, C to A, and AL4 to AL2 in Science.

He also specialises in coaching students to qualify for the School of Science & Technology (SST) via Direct School Admission (DSA). Notably, 100% of his SST Phase 2 candidates in 2024 qualified for 2025 SST admission.

As a parent of four children and a faithful Christian, Mr Yu brings sound parental wisdom and strong values to his teaching — a hallmark of an excellent and trusted Science educator.
